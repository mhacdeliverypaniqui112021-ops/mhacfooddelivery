MHAC DELIVERY V4.1.61 — STORE CLICK / COLLAPSE FOOD EDITOR

Food Editor flow:
- Store names are clickable accordion headers.
- Stores are collapsed by default to save vertical space.
- Example: Jollibee / KFC / McDo / Mang Inasal / Greenwich.
- Tap Jollibee: Jollibee opens and its categories + food names/prices/options appear immediately.
- Tap Jollibee again: it closes and the food list disappears.
- Only one store stays open at a time to keep the Admin page compact.
- Food/category editing and existing Firebase/menu publishing flow remain in place.
- Customer, Rider, and Business Time Control code are not intentionally changed by this UI patch.
