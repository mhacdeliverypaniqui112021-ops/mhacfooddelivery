SERVICE AREAS
PANIQUI - MONCADA - RAMOS - GERONA - PURA - ANAO - NAMPICUAN

The barangay lists in this package are based on Philippine Statistics Authority PSGC pages
(currently published through 31 July 2025).

LOCATION:
Customer can select Municipality + Barangay, enter house/street/landmark, and press
USE MY LOCATION. The browser GPS is used to get coordinates and a driving route is
requested from Paniqui Public Plaza. Delivery fee:
1st km = ₱40
Every succeeding km = +₱10
Distance is rounded up to the next whole kilometer for the fee calculation.

NOTE:
GPS permission is required. The route service is a public OSRM endpoint for testing.
If unavailable, the package falls back to straight-line distance.
