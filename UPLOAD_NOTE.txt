- One-box store/menu editor: Store Name, Category Name, Food Name, Base Price, Parts, Drinks, Add-ons, Original/Spicy selection backup.

- Customer places order -> NEW / WAITING FOR ADMIN.

- Countdown is shown to both Admin and Customer.
- Countdown never auto-completes or auto-cancels the order; zero means time expired but order remains active.

- Admin manually assigns rider and manually COMPLETES the order.
- Customer can CANCEL an active order before completion.
- Customer active order remains visible until COMPLETED (or customer cancellation).
- Completed orders disappear from active Admin and Customer lists.
- Daily earnings use completed orders for the current business day.
