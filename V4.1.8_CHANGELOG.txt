MHAC DELIVERY V4.1.8
AUTO CHAT RINGTONE + FOOD PART SELECTION FIX + MENU DRAFT RECOVERY

CHANGES
1. Admin ↔ Rider chat remains separate from Customer ↔ Admin chat.
2. Admin and Rider assignment/chat alerts now prepare automatically after login; no ENABLE SOUND button is required.
3. Customer chat ringtone also attempts automatic audio preparation; browser audio restrictions may still require the first normal user interaction.
4. Admin/Rider chatbox is smaller on mobile and moves above the Android keyboard.
5. Chicken part selection now uses per-part quantities:
   - 1x food: exactly one part piece.
   - 2x food: two pieces may be the same part or different parts.
   - 3x food: three pieces may be distributed across parts.
   Example: Leg 1, Thigh 2.
6. Drinks are optional in Food Editor. Copy Drinks stores them only in the clipboard; they are never automatically added to another food. Paste Drinks is required.
7. Pasting drinks replaces the target food's current drink list instead of duplicating it.
8. Admin menu draft recovery now compares local backup time with Firestore draft time so a newer food edit is not lost after refresh/deploy.
9. Build banner updated to V4.1.8.
10. Existing Customer ↔ Admin chat and direct Customer Cancel Order behavior preserved.
