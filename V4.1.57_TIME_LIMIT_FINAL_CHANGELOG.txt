MHAC DELIVERY V4.1.57 — FINAL BUSINESS TIME CONTROL

- Admin-only Business Time / Day Off controls.
- Admin remains accessible even when business is closed.
- Configurable OPEN and CLOSE time stored in settings/businessHours.
- Manual CLOSED / DAY OFF override stored in settings/businessHours.manualClosed.
- Customer app automatically blocks new ordering outside schedule or during manual Day Off.
- Customer app listens live for schedule/status changes.
- Rider app is not logged out or destroyed by business hours; existing assigned orders remain available for completion.
- Rider app shows CLOSED FOR NEW ASSIGNMENTS when closed, so active orders can still be completed.
- Existing order sync, chat, UI, and Firebase rider inbox flow preserved.
