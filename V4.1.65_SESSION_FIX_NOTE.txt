MHAC DELIVERY V4.1.65 SESSION FIX

Deep-check findings:
- Admin had no explicit auth persistence call, although Firebase web auth normally defaults to local persistence. V4.1.65 now explicitly sets browserLocalPersistence.
- Customer also explicitly sets browserLocalPersistence for consistency.
- Rider already used browserLocalPersistence, but its login flow explicitly called signOut(auth) whenever business hours were closed. That made a time-based business rule look like an automatic logout.
- Rider V4.1.65 no longer signs out when business hours end. Business hours now control new assignments only; the authenticated rider session remains active and existing assigned orders stay accessible.
- Admin remains accessible even when the business is closed.

UI/business rules were otherwise preserved.
